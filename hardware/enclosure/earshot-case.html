How it stays sealed

  • Magnet clip at the top: two 10 × 5 mm bar magnets in the top corners, either side of the antenna. It hangs from the top hem of a pocket (or a shirt), with a small plastic bar on the other side of the fabric that has magnets only at its ends. Hanging from the top keeps it upright and points the mic vents at the pocket opening.
  • O-ring between lid and base, clamped by four small screws.
  • Mic vents: two 1 mm vents, each covered inside by a waterproof acoustic membrane (ePTFE sticker, the kind phones use). Sound passes, water doesn't. A fully closed wall would muffle speech too much to transcribe.
  • Button: no hole. A thin flexible spot in the lid presses the switch through a plunger.
  • LED: no hole. It shines through a 0.5 mm thin patch of the lid.
  • USB-C: the one real opening, closed with a tethered silicone plug when not charging.

Rules that don't change

  • Antenna (blue box) is at the top too, so the magnets sit beside it, not under it, and the bar's middle is plastic. A phone nearby is well within range.
  • Weight is about 37 g (battery 20 g, case and magnets about 13 g, board 4 g), plus about 4 g for the bar. Use N52 magnets and test the hold on your pockets; thick fabric needs stronger ones.
  • Mic vents sit in the top end wall, pointing up toward your mouth. Sound reaches the board's two mic holes through the pocket beside the battery. Keep that end clear of jackets and scarves.
  • Program first. The Tag-Connect pads are used once to load the bootloader, before the case is closed.
  • Print dense. Hobby FDM prints leak through the layers; use 100% infill and a sealing coat, or order resin (SLA) or nylon (MJF) prints.
